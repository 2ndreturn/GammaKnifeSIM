⚛️ GAMMA KNIFE CHALLENGE

Move the lasers onto the tumor, choose laser count, hold fire, and click Done

🎮 How to Play

  1. 1. Move Lasers to Tumor: The tumor and your laser aim start in random spots. Drag or tap/click on the brain to move the laser convergence reticle onto the tumor, then click Lock Aim & Pick Lasers.
  2. 2. Pick Lasers (1–201): Fewer lasers take longer and cook the brain much faster if you keep firing too long.
  3. 3. Hold FIRE & Click Done: Hold FIRE for as long as you think it takes. Click Done when you want to finish and see if you won—if you over-fire and drain brain health, his brain is cooked!
💡 Real Physics: The Gamma Knife uses 201 beams so treatment takes only minutes. With fewer beams, you'd need to fire much longer, giving healthy tissue time to absorb damaging radiation. It's not that fewer beams can't kill the tumor—they just take longer, and that hurts the patient!

🎉 YOU WON!

You aimed onto the tumor, destroyed it to 0%, clicked Done in time, and kept the patient's brain alive.

💀 YOU LOST

Remember: align the lasers with the tumor, pick your laser count, hold FIRE as long as needed, and click Done before cooking the brain.
