⚛️ GAMMA KNIFE CHALLENGE

Destroy the brain tumor without harming the patient

🎮 How to Play

  1. Set your beam count (1-201). More beams = faster treatment + safer brain health.
  2. Press and hold FIRE to deliver radiation. Watch the tumor shrink!
  3. Release before brain health drops too low! Fewer beams means you must fire longer, exposing healthy brain to radiation.
  4. Win: Destroy the tumor (0%) while keeping brain health above 0%.
💡 Real Physics: The Gamma Knife uses 201 beams so treatment takes only minutes. With fewer beams, you'd need to fire much longer, giving healthy tissue time to absorb damaging radiation. It's not that fewer beams can't kill the tumor—they just take longer, and that hurts the patient!
