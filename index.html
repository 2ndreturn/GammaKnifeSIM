How Radiosurgery Works: Rather than surgically cutting or instantly destroying a tumor, stereotactic radiosurgery converges multiple narrow radiation beams at a precise 3D isocenter to halt cellular growth and prevent the lesion from expanding or invading surrounding brain tissue. Fewer beams require longer exposure times to achieve control, increasing radiation dose to adjacent healthy parenchyma.
